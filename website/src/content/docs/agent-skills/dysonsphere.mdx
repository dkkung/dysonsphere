The `dysonsphere` skill describes how to create, edit, and troubleshoot scientific figures with Dysonsphere and Altair.

## Behavior

- **Project setup:** follow project instructions and the existing data and plotting
  workflow before choosing code or dependencies.
- **Study design:** identify observational units, grouping, pairing, comparisons, and
  interval meanings before adding statistical or visual claims.
- **Chart construction:** use Altair charts with Dysonsphere themes, palettes,
  composite marks, annotations, and composition where they fit the task.
- **Verification:** execute the figure, render it through `ds.save()` or `ds.show()`, inspect the
  finished output, and report assumptions, checks, and output paths.

The skill points to [plotting.md](/agent-skills/references/plotting/) for chart design,
[statistics.md](/agent-skills/references/statistics/) for statistical annotations,
[utilities.md](/agent-skills/references/utilities/) for tables and offsets,
[nonlinear.md](/agent-skills/references/nonlinear/) for nonlinear axes, and
[export.md](/agent-skills/references/export/) for output and inspection.

It also instructs the agent to preserve data and analytical meaning, keep filters and transformations explicit, and stop when the requested figure is correct and readable.
